Tutorial

Four things to know before you drop in

Dodge is easy to read and hard to master. Here's everything the game assumes you already know.

01

Move, don't stop

Your square sits at the bottom of the field. Blocks fall from above — steer left and right to keep clear. On desktop use the keys below; on mobile, drag anywhere on the play field.

←→ or AD or drag to move
02

Getting hit costs points, not the run

Every mode is penalty-based. Touch a block and you lose points and your combo, not your life — you get a brief flash of invincibility to recover before the next one. Easier modes cost 20 points per hit; harder modes cost 15.

03

Every mode has a target

Reach the score shown next to each mode's name to clear it — that's a genuine win screen, not just running out the clock. Higher modes ask for a lot more, so the combo streak and bonus pickups start to matter.

04

Clear Abyss to find what's next

One mode stays hidden until you beat Abyss's target score. After that it's permanently unlocked in your mode list. No further hints here — go find it.

Field guide

What's falling, floating, and flashing

Some of these help you. Most of them don't. Learn to tell them apart at a glance.

■

Standard block

The core hazard. Colors vary by mode but the rule is the same — don't touch it.

★

Gold block

Rare, worth +40 on contact instead of a penalty. Same size as a normal block, different rules.

✦

Shield star

Absorbs your next hit completely — no point loss, no invincibility frame needed.

●

Slow-mo orb

Halves the speed of everything on screen for a few seconds. Use it to reset a bad position.

◐

Rewind clock

A straightforward +30 point pickup. Grab it in passing whenever the path lines up.

◉

Drone companion

Orbits your square and auto-clears the nearest close block. Stacks up to three levels.

▬

Laser sweep

Telegraphed with a dashed line before it fires solid. Appears in Inferno and above.

◆

Boss

Shows up once you've reached 40% of the target score in Inferno-tier modes. Drops volleys — treat it like a very persistent block.
